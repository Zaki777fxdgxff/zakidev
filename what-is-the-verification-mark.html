What is the verification mark?

Everything you need to know about the verification process, from the official documents required to the key differences between each badge type.

Verification on digital platforms is an administrative procedure aimed at confirming the identity of an account or the nature of its activity. It does not depend on the content the user publishes, but on the official documents they provide. In this guide, we will explain in detail the three types of verification badges, the official documents required for each, and how to apply.

Types of Verification Badges and Required Documents

Blue Badge (Standard Verification)

This badge is granted to any user after meeting the conditions, confirming that their identity as a person on the platform is real. This is the most common badge and is granted to individuals.

Official Documents Required:
  • Full Name.
  • Email Address.
  • Country Name.
  • ID Card Photo or Passport Photo.
  • Personal Photo.
Request Verification (Standard)
Gold Badge (Business Verification)

This badge is granted to companies that create an account on the platform, verifying them to confirm they are an official commercial entity.

Official Documents Required:
  • Full Name.
  • Email Address.
  • Country Name.
  • ID Card Photo or Passport Photo.
  • Personal Photo.
  • Photo of Commercial Register or Tax Card.
  • Company Address.
Request Verification (Business)
The brown mark (creator developer documentation)

This badge is dedicated to the app's founder and is not granted to any other entity. It is also granted to accounts affiliated with the app's founder, but the gold badge is often the most used for those affiliated accounts instead of the brown one.

There is no verification request for this badge; it is granted automatically by the platform administration.

Important Note: All submitted documents are subject to careful review by the verification team. Ensure photos are clear and data matches before submitting the request to avoid rejection or delay.

In the end, verification is an administrative step that ensures you get a badge that distinguishes your account. Choose the right type for your activity (personal or business) and prepare your required official documents to facilitate the verification process.
